About velorci

velorci is a complete business operating platform that runs a merchant's entire operation from a single dashboard, without stitching together separate tools. It is multi-tenant — every customer gets a fully isolated space with their own data, users, store and domain.

Who we build for

E-commerce merchants, delivery-dependent businesses, field teams of drivers, tailors and packers, and merchants selling through WhatsApp and Instagram who want their orders organized.

Built on

React frontend, Firebase data, serverless functions on Vercel, and image storage on Cloudflare R2 — works online on any device, no installation.

Why velorci

  • All-in-one: orders, store, accounting, WhatsApp, ads and POS in one subscription.
  • Full data isolation: every customer's data is completely separate.
  • No code needed: drag-and-drop store builder with your own domain.
  • Practical AI: WhatsApp agent, ad copywriter and dashboard insights.
  • Works on any device, no installation needed.
  • Full Arabic and English support, light and dark mode.
  • Flexible plans: turn any module on or off as you need.
  • Modern, reliable cloud architecture.
